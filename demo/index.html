Model. Liability = PGS + residual, total variance 1; disease if liability exceeds Φ⁻¹(1 − prevalence). PGS variance = R² (liability scale). Sibling embryos share the parental midpoint (variance R²/2) and differ by independent draws of variance R²/2. Ranking depends only on the sibling deviation, so the midpoint integrates out exactly: P(disease | deviation d) = Φ((√(R²/2)·d − T)/√(1 − R²/2)). Expected risk for the best and worst of n embryos is integrated over the min and max order-statistic densities. This is the approach of Lencz et al. (eLife 2021) cited by the preprint, and it reproduces the preprint's 2, 5 and 10 embryo table to within 1 point.

Sources. 2026 table: "UK Biobank Validation of Nucleus Models", mynucleus.com/labs (read Nov 12, 2026). 2025 preprint: Cordogan et al., "Within- and Between-Family Validation of Nine Polygenic Risk Scores Developed in 1.5 Million Individuals", medRxiv 10.1101/2025.10.24.25338613, Supplementary Table 2 (lifetime prevalence, liability R² and 95% CI) and Table 3 (within-family beta ratio). Within-family mode for the preprint follows the paper: only type 1 diabetes is reduced, by the beta ratio squared (0.827²). The uncertainty range is shown only for preprint inputs, using the published liability R² 95% CI; prevalence is held fixed. No CI is published for the 2026 table, so none is shown.
